☀️ Warm-up 5 min

Responda em inglês. Frases curtas valem! Clique para ver ideias de resposta.

1. Have you ever talked to a stranger in a line?
Yes, I have. / No, I haven’t. / Sometimes.
2. Do you like talking to new people?
Yes, I do. / No, I don’t. / It depends. I’m shy. 😅
3. What do you usually say when you need help?
Excuse me, can you help me? / Sorry, I need help.
4. Is small talk common in Brazil?
Yes, it is. People talk a lot in lines. / Not really.
💡 Small talk é uma conversa curta e informal, como comentar a fila ou o tempo. Você não precisa saber todas as palavras, só precisa saber continuar a conversa.

🏦 Situation 1: At the bank 12 min

Primeiro só ouça (livro fechado). Depois leia junto.

AnnaExcuse me, is this the line for the bank?
TomYes, it is. The line is very long today!
AnnaOh no. How long do I need to wait?
TomAbout twenty minutes, I think.
AnnaThat’s a long time! I need to pay a bill.
TomMe too. Is this your first time here?
AnnaYes, it is. Is it easy?
TomYes, don’t worry. The people here are nice.
AnnaGreat, thank you! Have a nice day!
TomYou too. Good luck!
🧩 Estrutura: começar (Excuse me, …) → continuar (comentário + pergunta) → encerrar (Thank you! Have a nice day!)

❓ Comprehension

Where are Anna and Tom?
At the bank, in a line.
How long do they wait?
About twenty minutes.
Why is Anna at the bank?
She needs to pay a bill.

🔁 Substitution drill

  • The line is very long → short / slow / crowded
  • I need to pay a bill → ask a question / open an account
  • How long do I need to wait? → stay / be here

🎭 Roleplay: troque de papel (Anna ↔ Tom) e depois faça sem olhar o texto, mudando um detalhe.

💊 Situation 2: At the pharmacy 11 min

Vocabulário sempre dentro de uma frase.

WordSignificadoExample
🤒 I don’t feel wellNão estou me sentindo bemExcuse me, I don’t feel well.
🤕 headachedor de cabeçaI have a headache.
😷 cough · sore throattosse · dor de gargantaI have a cough and a sore throat.
🌡️ feverfebreI have a fever.
💊 medicineremédioDo you have medicine for a headache?
📄 prescriptionreceita médicaDo I need a prescription?
⏰ twice a dayduas vezes ao diaTake one pill twice a day.

🗣️ Mini-dialogue

RicardoExcuse me. I don’t feel well.
PharmacistOh, I’m sorry. What’s the problem?
RicardoI have a headache and a fever.
PharmacistOkay. Take one pill twice a day, after meals.
RicardoDo I need a prescription?
PharmacistNo, you don’t. Anything else?
RicardoNo, thank you.
How many pills? When?
One pill, twice a day, after meals.

🎭 Variações: troque o sintoma · o farmacêutico fala rápido (use Sorry, can you repeat that?) · você esqueceu a palavra (use I’m not sure, but…).

🛒 Situation 3: At the supermarket extra

Perguntar onde fica um produto, saber o preço e pedir ajuda.

WordSignificadoExample
🛒 aislecorredorThe milk is in aisle three.
🗄️ shelfprateleiraIt’s on the top shelf.
💰 How much is it?Quanto custa?Excuse me, how much is this milk?
🏷️ expensive · cheapercaro · mais baratoThis is expensive. Is there a cheaper one?
🙋 Where is…?Onde fica…?Excuse me, where is the milk?

🗣️ Mini-dialogue

RicardoExcuse me, where is the milk?
EmployeeIt’s in aisle three, next to the cheese.
RicardoThank you. How much is this milk?
EmployeeIt’s six reais.
RicardoHmm, that’s expensive. Is there a cheaper one?
EmployeeYes, this one is cheaper. It’s five reais.
RicardoGreat, I’ll take it. Thanks for your help!
EmployeeYou’re welcome!
Where is the milk? How much is the cheaper one?
In aisle three, next to the cheese. The cheaper one is five reais.

🎭 Variações: troque o produto (bread, rice, eggs) · o funcionário fala rápido (use Could you speak more slowly?) · você não encontra o produto (use Can you help me, please?).

🎯 Speaking Challenge 12 min

“What would you say in this situation?” Pense 10–15 segundos, tente, e só então veja a pista e a resposta-modelo.

  • ❓ You don’t understand the pharmacist. What do you say?
  • 🎭 Encene uma conversa curta de fila sem ler o diálogo.
